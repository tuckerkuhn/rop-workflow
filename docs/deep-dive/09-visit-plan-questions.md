# Visits against a plan: questions for the agronomists

Before the app plans farm visits a week ahead, the agronomists who run the field teams should
answer these. Their answers decide what the plan holds, what "behind" means, and what the technical
lead's week should say. Ask them in person or in a short call; 20 minutes is enough.

## Monday morning

1. What do you look at on a Monday to plan the week's visits?
   a) A list of farmers by village. b) Where each farmer is in the season. c) Last week's visits.
   d) Something else (what?).
2. Who decides which farmers each extension agent visits: you, the agent, or the project manager?
3. How many visits can one agent do in a day, on foot, by motorbike, by car?

## Falling behind

4. How do you know an agent is falling behind today?
5. How do you know a farmer is falling behind (late planting, missed training, a problem not followed up)?
6. When a planned visit does not happen, what should happen next: moved to next week, flagged
   to you, or nothing?

## What you wish you heard sooner

7. What do you find out too late today?
8. What would you want the app to tell you on Monday, in one sentence?

## The plan itself

9. Is a week the right length, or do you plan by fortnight or by crop stage?
10. Should a farm visit in KoboCollect be matched to a planned visit automatically (same farmer,
    same week), or should the agent pick the plan line?
11. Who may change a plan once the week has started?

## What the app does with the answers

- A weekly visit plan per agent, kept as a project_state document (no new tables).
- The technical lead's week says which planned visits did not happen and which farmers have gone
  longest without one.
- KoboCollect stays the only record of a visit (owner's decision, 23 Nov 2026): the plan says what
  should happen, Kobo says what did.
