# ROP Workflow: Review Round 2 (internal)

Reviewed 28 Nov 2026 against the local copy only (http://localhost:3100, throwaway database, demo sign-in by the `x-demo-user` header). Demo project A-2027 unless a row says otherwise. Same severity scale as round 1:

- Critical: lets money move or a record pass without the required independent check, loses data, or blocks a workflow.
- High: weakens a required control, or gives wrong numbers that another check may still catch.
- Medium: inconsistent data, friction or missing guidance that people can work around.
- Low: wording or display.

All requests below are `curl -s -H "x-demo-user: <id>" -H "content-type: application/json" localhost:3100<path> -d '<body>'`. "PUT procurement" means: GET `/api/projects/A-2027/procurements`, take the record, change what the row says, and PUT it back as `{"items":[record]}` (the screen does the same). The helper used is in the scratchpad (`rev/proc.py`). `R` is an uploaded receipt `{"hash":"69abe2b4...8f33","name":"r2receipt.pdf","type":"application/pdf","size":60}` (POST `/api/files`).

## 1. Results by scenario

**S1 Farm input distribution.** Request, requisition and goods received now work by hand (no AI needed). The goods received note can only be signed by the requester (Laila and Farid refused, Nadia accepted), and the stock check counts it by date. Distribution approval is held for farmer screening and for the receipt check, and the recorder cannot approve their own hand-out. What did not hold: the screening hold is lifted by the approver alone, who can record a possible match and then clear it herself with a 2019 date (R2-10). Large purchases cannot be signed at all: the CEO, who must sign above $25,000, has view-only Procurement access (R2-01).

**S2 Procurement.** Tier routing holds (Karim cannot sign a Large approval). A request on A-2027 does not have to come from the calendar, so the calendar's two approvals and its early split warning are skipped there (R2-05). The requester can override a budget shortfall herself with a tick, no reason and no funder document (R2-03). The contract value is never compared with the requisition, the chosen bid or the budget line (R2-04). Payment still comes after goods receipt in the procurement flow, but Record a payment lets Finance pay the supplier of an open purchase outside it (R2-06, R2-07).

**S3 Segregation of duties.** Holds: bank reconciliation (Yusuf and Omid refused for a month they posted in), calendar seats (asker cannot approve), voucher steps, field records (Omar cannot accept his own farm visit), verification visits (collector refused), advances approval (not self). Fails: the procurement officer can make herself technical lead on a calendar item and approve it, so procurement signs both seats (R2-02); a person with bill-entry rights but no posting right posts a cash payment to the books alone (R2-08); a Finance officer pays his own advance (R2-09).

**S4 Audit replay.** #1 advances: built, but Finance can pay itself, a new advance is allowed while the last one still owes money, and advances never reach the books or the payment accounts register (R2-09, R2-14, R2-15). #2 wallet in a personal name: the register refuses a personal owner and needs a second person to confirm; holds. #3 contract above its budget line: not caught (R2-04). #4 missing documents: still caught. #5 third-party payee: needs authorization and a screening date, but both are only typed or attached by the payer, the same file can serve as receipt and authorization, and "paid via" wording slips past (R2-11, R2-12). Duplicate payments with the same receipt post without a warning (R2-17).

**S5 Backup approvers.** Recording time away works, and for field records and distributions the delegate gets the roles while the recorder still cannot approve their own record. For procurement the screen gives the delegate the approver role, but the server refuses the signature, so an approval waits while its holder is away (R2-13). HR approvals (advances, and by the same code timesheets) are not delegated at all (R2-16). Anyone on the staff can be named as delegate, including the person whose work is waiting (R2-21, from code).

**S6 M&E.** Household roster, duplicate check by phone and by name spelling, training scores and the monthly verification sample work; the verifier cannot be the collector. Farmer IDs are case sensitive, so `f-r2-01` and `F-R2-01` are two farmers (R2-18). Per-package rules refuse ticking a rule the record fails, but one person can select the farmer anyway as an exception (R2-20). Bulk acceptance trusts the browser's list of "opened" records: Omar accepted 11 results by naming two of them as opened (R2-19).

**S7 HR.** Timesheets hold (no submission before 30 Nov; long days need a note; a 20-hour day with a note is accepted, as decided in D-24). Staff screening holds a hire, but HR can clear its own possible match at once (R2-10). Recruitment rules are in place, but the posting date is typed, a shortlist of 3 to 5 can end up with fewer, and a hire can be proposed with no vacancy at all (R2-22, R2-23, from code). Panels cannot sit until grades are recorded (Needs Tucker 19, not re-reported).
