## The one finding that matters

**Field work never reached Progress, the monthly review or the report.** A manager approved a distribution, the screen said "it now counts toward the distribution target", and nothing counted. On the demo project the Check section opened empty: no targets, no budget, no activities. That is fixed below (commits 5 and 6).

## Decisions that are yours. I picked the cautious default for each.

1. **Should a manager-approved distribution count at once, or wait for MEL?** I made it wait, because every other result waits and the Results page can accept clean ones in one tap. Counting at once is one line if you prefer it.
2. **Distributions from KoboCollect do not use the new route.** They count through the form's own link to a target. That route is not gated by the manager's approval: a distribution she sent back can still be accepted by MEL. Worth deciding which check wins.
3. **Training, readiness and visits still do not make results.** Only distributions do. Same question, three more times.
4. **The budget line mapping (seed on 2.1, NPK on 2.2) is unchanged.** The demo budget uses those codes because the demo purchases already did. It decides nothing for a real project.
5. **Names on the voucher, Dari and Pashto labels:** not touched.

## Things I found and did not fix

- A MEL officer cannot accept a result they recorded. If MEL records a readiness visit, nobody can accept it unless a second person holds the MEL role. The demo avoids this. A real team of 1 MEL officer will hit it.
- A purchase finds its activity by matching words between the budget line and the workplan. It works on the demo because I worded the demo to match. A purchase should name its activity.
- The procurement calendar on the demo still says "Build it from the budget". That is one tap for the procurement manager, so I left it as a demo step.
- Home still makes about 22 requests on open.
- Nothing here was run as a production build in my sandbox. Vercel will be the first.
