## Decisions that are yours. I picked the cautious default for each.

1. **Should a manager-approved distribution count at once, or wait for MEL?** I made it wait, because every other result waits and the Results page can accept clean ones in one tap. Counting at once is one line if you prefer it.
2. **Who approves KoboCollect data: now a setting (commit 11), and the default is unchanged.** Until you choose on each project, anyone with the MEL role approves, as before. To get the rule you described, pick "One named person" and name the Director of MEL and a stand-in. The app knows who collected a record once each KoboCollect account is matched to a person on the KoboToolbox setup screen (commit 16). Until an account is matched, the named person could approve a record they collected themselves, so match every account, or name people who do not collect.
3. **All field data comes through KoboCollect (decided 15 Nov).** The repo has forms for readiness and distribution. Training and visit forms are still to build. Field activities still lets people record steps by hand; turning that into a view-only screen is a later change.
4. **The budget line mapping (seed on 2.1, NPK on 2.2) is unchanged.** The demo budget uses those codes because the demo purchases already did. It decides nothing for a real project.
5. **Names on the voucher, Dari and Pashto labels:** not touched.

## Things I found and did not fix

- A named approver needs field access switched on to open the Results page. A director with no field access can check distributions but not accept results.

- The collector rule needs each KoboCollect account matched to a person. Records sent before this change by forms that feed results directly do not carry their account until they are synced again. The rule is tested as logic; I have not clicked through the matching screen in a browser.
- Staff expense reports still carry a funding source and an account code but no budget line. Closing that needs Finance to map each expense category to a budget line per project, so staff never have to choose. Not built.
- Purchases made before commit 18 keep the row the app matched. Nobody is asked to go back and confirm them.
- The procurement calendar on the demo still says "Build it from the budget". That is one tap for the procurement manager, so I left it as a demo step.
- Home still makes about 22 requests on open.
- Nothing here was run as a production build in my sandbox. Vercel will be the first.
